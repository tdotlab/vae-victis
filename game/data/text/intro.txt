Six years ago, Gaius Julius Caesar came to Gaul to protect it from the Helvetii. He has been protecting it ever since. He has broken the Belgae and the Veneti, burned his way across the Rhenus and back, and sold more Gauls into slavery than anyone can count. Most peoples made their peace with him. Some got rich.

Five years ago, when Rome's reach came as far as the Cadurci, your village of Brigodunon swore friendship to Rome, and gave three children as a pledge of it. One of them was your sister, Eponina. She was nine.

This winter, Caesar is in Italy, and the snow is deep on the passes. In the forest of the Carnutes, the chiefs of Gaul have sworn an oath over their gathered war standards. Last night a rider came through the hills shouting the news from Cenabum.

The revolt has begun. And Rome has three children from Brigodunon.
