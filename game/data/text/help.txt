Left-click the ground to walk. Left-click a person to talk to them, a chest to open it, a body to search it. Right-click anything to look at it.

Fighting is turn-based. A fight starts when someone hostile sees you, or when you press A. Click an enemy to attack it. Each turn you have action points (AP), shown as studs on the bar. Moving costs AP per hex (more in mud and brush); attacking costs what the weapon says. Right-click an enemy to aim at a body part. AP you don't spend make you harder to hit. Space ends your turn; Enter ends the fight once no enemy is close.

Shields stop blows that come from the front. A javelin that hits a shield can stick in it and make it useless for the rest of the fight.

Keys: Escape menu and this help, I gear, C character, J journal, M travel, S sneak, A start a fight, F5 quicksave, F9 quickload, arrow keys move the view, mouse wheel zooms, Home recenters.

On the world map, click a place you know (on the map or in the list) to travel there, or click open country to walk toward it. Right-click or Space stops. E enters the place you're standing on, R rests, Home recenters. Open country is faster than forest, hills and marsh, and the days pass as you walk: news of the war will find you on the road.

Saving: the menu has six save slots besides the quicksave. The game also autosaves each time you arrive somewhere, though never into a fight on the road. Continue on the title loads whichever save is newest.
