The tally was cut at Noviodunum, on the Liger: Caesar's depot in the country of the Aedui, where he keeps his baggage, his remounts, his pay chest, and the children of every people in Gaul who promised to behave.

It is two hundred miles north, through a country going to war. The Arverni are rising behind Vercingetorix. The Bituriges will burn their own towns rather than feed the legions, all but one. Caesar is coming back over the mountains in the snow. And somewhere in Noviodunum, in a low building by the river behind the horse lines, Eponina is fourteen years old and learning to say yes in Latin.
